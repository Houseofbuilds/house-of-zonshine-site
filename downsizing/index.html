Downsizing

Downsizing in Los Angeles: how to plan your next move.

A thoughtful plan for deciding what matters, preparing your current home, and finding the right next fit in Los Angeles.

By Julia ZonshineLos Angeles Real Estate
“Downsizing is not about having less. It is about keeping what makes your life work.”

Yes—Julia Zonshine helps Los Angeles homeowners downsize by turning a big, emotional change into a clear plan for the current home, the next home, and the timing in between.

Begin with what you want more of.

More ease, less maintenance, a home without stairs, a closer-in location, or simply a different rhythm are all valid reasons to move.

Make space for a thoughtful sale.

Preparing a long-loved home can take care and time. I help sellers identify what is worth doing before market and create a plan for the home’s presentation.

Downsizing questions

When should I start planning a downsizing move?

Earlier than you think. The smoothest plans leave time to understand the current home’s value, decide what stays and goes, prepare the home, and find the next place without unnecessary pressure.

Does downsizing mean giving up the things I love about my home?

No. It is about deciding what matters most in the next chapter, not simply reducing square footage.
