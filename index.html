Meet Julia

I know how personal this decision actually is.

I’m Julia Zonshine, a Los Angeles real estate agent at Compass, a mother of two, and a Sherman Oaks local who notices the architecture, the block, and all the little details that make a house feel different once you’re standing inside it.

Before real estate, I lived in Japan and somehow became the face model for Jill Valentine in Resident Evil, which is still a sentence I never expected to be part of my professional biography. Now I help people make some of the biggest decisions of their lives, and I try to make the process feel a lot less intimidating and a lot more human.

My clients should always feel informed, supported, and like someone is genuinely looking out for them.

The Sycamore House · $2,820,000

I sold Christopher Lloyd’s house, and lost my mind a little.

A few months ago I helped sell the Sycamore House, the Hollywood home Christopher Lloyd lovingly transformed and made his own. He left an actual mould of his face somewhere in that house. I grew up watching Back to the Future more times than I can count, so Little Julia would think present-day Julia is pretty cool.

Glendale · Sold

East Glenoaks Boulevard · $2,000,000

A buyer's win in Glendale.

I represented the buyer on this one, and even after we closed I stayed on top of the details that mattered, chasing down a fire hazard disclosure issue with escrow so my buyer never had to think twice about it. Funny enough, that buyer was Diva, the same client I later helped sell her Sycamore home. I love when it works out that way.
