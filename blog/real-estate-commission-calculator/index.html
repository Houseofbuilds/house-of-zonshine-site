Real Estate Commission Calculator

Enter any sale price and see the gross commission before team splits, brokerage fees, marketing costs, and taxes ever enter the picture.

By Julia Zonshine September 11, 2026

Commission math sounds simple until a million-dollar house makes the number feel enormous, and then it is very easy to confuse gross commission with what an agent actually takes home.

Here in LA, 2.5% on one side is a rate I see often, so I used it as the starting point below, but commission is always negotiable and you can adjust the rate to match the number you are actually working with. This calculator keeps the math intentionally simple: sale price times rate equals gross commission on one side of the transaction.
