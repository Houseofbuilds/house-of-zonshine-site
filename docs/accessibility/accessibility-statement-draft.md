# Accessibility statement — owner-approved website copy

> Owner approved for website publication on August 24, 2026. This statement is a commitment and limitation disclosure, not a certification or WCAG conformance claim. Legal review and an independent production audit remain recommended.

## Our commitment

House of Zonshine is committed to making our website and digital services usable by people with disabilities. We are working toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

Our work includes accessibility testing during development, remediation of identified barriers, keyboard and responsive testing, accessible content and document practices, staff guidance, and periodic independent review. We do not rely on an accessibility overlay as a substitute for accessible source code and content.

## Need help or another format?

Please tell us the page or document involved, what you were trying to do, and the format or assistance that would work for you. We will make reasonable efforts to provide timely, effective access through an alternative method while we investigate the underlying issue.

## Known limitations

As of August 24, 2026, our downloadable PDF guides are undergoing a separate document-accessibility audit. The Los Angeles relocation guide is known to need structural remediation. Until a remediated version is available, contact us and we will provide the information or assistance in an accessible alternative format.

Some supplemental maps and external listing/social pages are operated by third parties. If one of those services creates a barrier, contact us so we can provide the relevant House of Zonshine information or help through another route and report the issue to the provider.

## Feedback and review

We welcome accessibility feedback and use it to prioritize improvements. This statement was last reviewed on August 24, 2026.
