Los Angeles relocation

Relocating to Los Angeles: what to know before you move.

A local, candid partner for choosing where to live, evaluating a home from afar, and arriving with a clear picture of the life you are building.

By Julia ZonshineLos Angeles Real Estate
“My job is to help Los Angeles feel familiar—like home—before you arrive.”

Yes—I help people relocate to Los Angeles by narrowing down where to live, being a local set of eyes during the search, and giving an honest picture of a home and its neighborhood before they arrive. My job is to help Los Angeles feel familiar—like home—before you even get here.

Los Angeles is not one experience. The Valley, the Eastside, Northeast LA, the Westside, Pasadena, and the hills all make a different kind of daily life possible. I have lived in several versions of this city myself, and I work with buyers across it, so we can start with how you want your life to feel—not just a list of bedrooms or a pin on a map.

Start with the part of LA that fits your actual life.

This is where my neighborhood guides started. Clients were reaching out wanting to understand the nuances between different parts of Los Angeles, so I put that perspective online. Whether you are already working with me or simply trying to make more sense of LA, the guides give you a place to start.

Before we get deep into listings, we talk through your routine, what you want nearby, how much driving feels reasonable, what kind of home matters to you, and where your budget will stretch. Then I help you turn that into a search that has some shape to it instead of trying to learn all of Los Angeles at once. The Guide Library is a good place to begin, especially the Relocating to Los Angeles guide and the neighborhood guides that explain how different areas actually live.

What I do when you cannot be here for every showing.

When you are searching from out of town, I go deeper than opening the door and holding up a phone. I am measuring square footage, turning every faucet on and off, looking closely at the condition, walking the street and the block, and paying attention to the small things that will matter once you are actually living there. I want you to know what is there—not just what photographed well.

That is how I found one client a single-story Woodland Hills home without them ever stepping foot inside before moving in. It is also how I found another client a very specific, architecturally significant mid-century home in Silver Lake, and helped make it feel familiar and like home before they even arrived.

Proof matters more than a promise.

Read the Woodland Hills relocation story and the Silver Lake mid-century relocation story, both about clients who needed a clear, local picture before they arrived. If you are early in the process, I am happy to start with the question that is usually the hardest one: where in LA should I actually be looking?

Relocating to Los Angeles: common questions

Can Julia help if I am moving to Los Angeles from out of state?

Yes. I help relocating buyers narrow down where to live, evaluate homes and neighborhoods from afar, and make decisions with a clearer local picture before they arrive.

Can I buy a Los Angeles home without being at every showing?

Yes, especially if you are out of town for inspections or other big moments. Communication is key for me, so I use FaceTime, detailed walkthroughs, and useful AI tools to help you really see and feel the home without being at every showing.
