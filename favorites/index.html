The Zonshine Edit

This might be
an obsession.

Some houses are beautiful. The special ones feel alive. After you’ve walked through enough of them, you start to recognize the difference—the original detail that survived every renovation, the strange little choice that gives a room its soul, the way a house seems to know exactly what it is.

These are the houses that spoke to me. They may be my listings or someone else’s, modest or extraordinary. The only requirement is that something about them was impossible to forget.
