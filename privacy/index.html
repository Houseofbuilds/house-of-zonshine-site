Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information this website collects, why it is collected, and the choices available to you.

Effective July 24, 2026

Who operates this website

This website is operated by Julia Zonshine under the personal brand House of Zonshine. Julia is a California real estate agent affiliated with Compass. Questions about this policy can be sent to julia.zonshine@compass.com.

Information collected

The website may collect the following information when you choose to provide it:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your real estate interest, such as buying or selling
  • Information you include in an optional message
  • Your newsletter subscription request

The website’s hosting and form providers may also process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, and request logs to deliver, secure, and maintain the site.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to your inquiry, provide requested real estate information, send the newsletter you requested, maintain and secure the website, prevent spam or misuse, and comply with legal obligations. Newsletter subscriptions and real estate inquiries are treated as separate requests.

How information may be shared

Information may be processed by service providers that support the website, including Netlify for hosting and form processing and any email or newsletter provider connected to the site. Information may also be shared with Compass, Julia’s real estate team, or professional service providers when reasonably necessary to respond to your request or provide services you requested. Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect legal rights and site security.

House of Zonshine does not currently sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Cookies and tracking

The current website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or behavioral tracking. Hosting, security, embedded media, font, or other service providers may process standard technical requests when their services load. If analytics, advertising technology, or additional cookies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated.

Because the current site does not engage in cross-site behavioral tracking, it does not maintain a separate response to browser “Do Not Track” signals. Where legally applicable, recognized opt-out preference signals will be respected.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from newsletter emails using the unsubscribe method included in each email. You may also ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted through this site by emailing julia.zonshine@compass.com. Some information may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, compliance, security, or recordkeeping purposes.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used, but no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for adults seeking real estate information and is not directed to children under 13. House of Zonshine does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

External links

This site may link to Compass, social platforms, or other third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website’s services change. The effective date at the top will be revised when material updates are made.
