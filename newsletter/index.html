Once a month · From Julia

Stories about houses, people, and Los Angeles.

This is a different kind of real estate email. Sometimes it begins inside a remarkable house, and sometimes it begins with a person who makes one little stretch of Los Angeles feel like a small town.

What to Expect

Personal first, useful always.

One of the greatest gifts of this business is getting a front-row seat to people’s stories, and the newsletter is where I get to slow down enough to tell some of them properly.

You’ll also find market perspective, a few genuinely interesting things happening around Los Angeles, neighborhood discoveries, design and architecture, and the kind of useful real estate information I would send to a friend. It arrives monthly, usually within the first week, after the first-of-the-month rush has cleared out a little.

Joining the newsletter simply means you want to read it. It is completely separate from requesting a real estate consultation.
